# Sum Insured Limits

## Major Medical Illness

- Options of 9 / 19 / 52 critical illnesses
- Pays lump sum sum insured if the insured person is diagnosed with a listed major medical illness
- Angioplasty:
  - 25% of the major medical illness sum insured
  - Maximum INR 10 Lakhs

---

## Loss of Job - Termination of Employment

- Up to 3 EMIs payable in the event of termination from employment
- Applicable to both insured persons

---

## Permanent Disablement

Pays according to the benefit table if the insured person sustains an injury resulting in permanent disablement.

---

## Accidental Death

- Pays 100% of sum insured if insured person sustains accidental injury
- Death must occur within 12 months of the accident
- Includes disappearance and comatose cover

---

## Dependent Child Education Benefit

- Pays from INR 25,000 to INR 5 Lakhs
- Covers dependent children’s education
- Maximum 2 children
- Applicable to both insured persons

---

# EMI Hospitalisation

Pays Equated Monthly Instalments (EMI) / sum insured for hospitalisation.

## Limits & Details

| Particulars | Limits / Details |
|---|---|
| Sum insured | 1% of Accidental Death sum insured |
| Payout frequency | Based on hospitalisation duration |
| 1st payout | 5 days |
| 2nd payout | 7 days |
| 3rd payout | 9 days |
| 4th payout | 11 days |
| Maximum annual payout | 4 times |

---

# Vector Borne Disease Cover

Pays INR 50,000 for medically necessary hospitalisation due to:

- Dengue Fever
- Malaria
- Chikungunya
- Japanese Encephalitis
- Kala-azar
- Lymphatic Filariasis
- Zika Virus

---

# Loss Of Income - Major Medical Illness

- Covers 19 / 52 critical illnesses
- Pays up to 50% of annual salary
- Equivalent to 6 times monthly salary

## Multi-individual Policies

- 50% of 6 times monthly salary for each insured person

## Floater Policies

- 6 times monthly salary of highest earning member

---

# Home Building & Contents Cover

Covers loss or damage to the structure and contents of the house due to:

- Fire
- Earthquake
- Storm
- Flood
- Landslide
- Inundation
- Allied perils

## Notes

- Coverage up to loan tenure
- Terrorism excluded
- Home content cover restricted to 10% of structure sum insured

---

# Burglary & Housebreaking Including Larceny & Theft

- Covers home contents
- Jewellery kept in safe covered up to 20% of burglary section sum insured
- Claim payable on 40% first-loss basis
- Offered for 5-year tenure

# Claims Payout

## Fixed Basis

Compensation under:

- Accidental Death
- Permanent Disablement
- Major Medical Illness
- Loss of Job
- Loss of Income
- EMI Hospitalisation

will first satisfy the outstanding loan amount, and the remaining amount will be paid to nominee/insured.

---

## Reducing Basis

Compensation will first be paid towards the outstanding loan amount.
